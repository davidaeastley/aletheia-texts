Aletheia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Aletheia is a scripture study app. This page describes what it does and does not do with your data.

No account, no tracking

Aletheia does not require an account, does not include analytics or crash-reporting software, and does not show ads. Nothing you read, search for, highlight, or write in the app is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

Your notes, highlights, annotations, search history, reading position, and settings are stored only in the app's local database and local storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you use the app's own backup/export feature, the resulting file is saved where you choose and shared only if you choose to share it.

Internet access

Aletheia uses the internet only to download content you ask for: additional scripture translations, dictionaries, or other text packs, and the Journal of Discourses. These downloads come from a public GitHub repository and do not include any information about you.

Local network features

The Scripture Quiz's multiplayer mode works over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi only — it does not use the internet and does not send data to the developer. Android requires the app to request Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions (and, on some Android versions, location permission) to offer this feature; Aletheia does not read, store, or transmit your location.

On-device text recognition

The "My Patriarchal Blessing" feature can read text from a PDF you choose, using an on-device recognition model bundled with the app. The PDF and the recognized text never leave your device.
